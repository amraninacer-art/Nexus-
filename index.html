06 — THE HOUSE

BORN INALGIERS.

Made for everywhere.

NEXUS began with a refusal: that premium fashion had to be imported to be believed. Algeria has light, geometry, rhythm and memory — everything a modern house needs. We cut our first patterns in Algiers in 2024 and have not looked away since.

Our vocabulary is drawn from the city itself — the white terraces, the deep blue of the Mediterranean at dusk, the olive of the hills, the burnt clay of the south. Every garment is an edit: remove until only identity remains.

Identity is not static, so neither are we. NEXUS is where heritage meets movement — delivered to all 58 wilayas, worn beyond the ordinary.
